Dear Editors of the Chinese/English Journal of Educational Measurement and Evaluation,

Please consider our manuscript, "How Large Language Model Benchmarks Are Built: A Measurement-Oriented Tutorial and Empirical Demonstration with IRT-Viz-Eval," as an illustrative methodological tutorial with a reproducible two-model demonstration.

The manuscript addresses a practical problem for educational measurement researchers: large language model benchmarks are often discussed as though they were conventional tests, even though the evaluated object, administration conditions, response process, exposure risks, scoring mechanisms, and maintenance cycle differ substantially. The article translates benchmark construction into an eight-stage evidence-building workflow for an applied measurement audience. IRT-Viz-Eval illustrates the workflow with exact mathematical ground truth, controlled visual transformations, diagnostic baselines, and complete 198-task administrations of GLM-4.5V and Gemma 3 4B through Hugging Face Inference Providers. Their nearly identical overall scores conceal marked differences by task type, output completeness, latency, and aggregation rule.

We believe the manuscript fits CEJEME's emphasis on methodological, illustrative, empirical, and review articles relevant to educational measurement in an international and technology-driven context. It is intended to help measurement researchers evaluate AI claims and design transparent model studies, rather than to present a transient leaderboard.

The manuscript is original, has not been published, is not under review elsewhere, and has been approved by all authors. The uploaded main document is anonymized for double-blind review. Code and generated benchmark materials can be supplied as an anonymized supplement; the public repository link will be added after review.
